FAQ

Common questions.

Does e-invoicing apply to my business?

The mandate covers business-to-business and business-to-government transactions by businesses in the UAE. Businesses with revenue below AED 50 million must appoint an accredited service provider by 31 March 2027 and go live by 1 July 2027. A readiness check confirms exactly where you stand.

Are you an accredited service provider?

No. Accredited service providers run the platforms that send and validate e-invoices. Fifth System is an implementation partner: we help you choose and appoint a provider, then get your systems and data working with it.

My accounting software says it's e-invoicing ready. Do I still need help?

Possibly not much — but software readiness is only part of it. Most problems come from data: missing or wrong TRNs, inconsistent customer records, credit notes, and invoices raised outside the main system. A readiness check tells you quickly.

Can you work with us remotely?

Yes. Delivery runs over video calls, shared workspaces and your systems, with working hours that overlap the UAE business day.

How much does it cost?

Every package is fixed-scope and quoted in writing after a short call, based on your systems, invoice volumes and how many places invoices are created.
